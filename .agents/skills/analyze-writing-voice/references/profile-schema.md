# Voice Profile Schema

Keep the canonical profile compact enough to load during writing tasks.

## Required sections

1. **Status** — version, date, intended uses, evidence scope, and overall confidence
2. **Stable voice** — posture, reasoning, reader relationship, and certainty
3. **Observable style** — diction, syntax, rhythm, structure, rhetoric, and humor
4. **Registers** — what remains stable, what changes, common failures, and confidence
5. **Preserve** — characteristics editing commonly removes
6. **Avoid** — evidenced anti-patterns and their effects
7. **Mechanics** — house conventions kept separate from voice
8. **Evidence summary** — strongest support, contradiction, and provenance limitations
9. **Unresolved questions**
10. **Change history**

Use `high`, `medium`, or `low` confidence. Mark unsupported dimensions as unresolved rather than
inventing a preference.
